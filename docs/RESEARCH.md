# Research

## Prior art

The Pac-Man and Snake combination already exists. The nearest public examples found on
2026-11-20 are [Pac-Snake](https://kuroo-kyo.itch.io/pac-snake),
[SNEKMAN](https://funkypotato.com/snekman/), and
[Pac-Snec](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4048060). A Processing project named
[Pac-Snake](https://github.com/ivzhang1/Pac-Snake) also exists.

Mawline therefore does not claim the mash-up as novel. Its specific design focus is a
pixel-grown, strategically edible tail, a power-state tail-pruning choice, and a
length-time survival score in original maze layouts.
